# instacart-customer-analysis
Instacart Customer Analysis explores online grocery data to uncover shopping patterns, customer segments, and product demand. Using Python, Excel, and Tableau, the project delivers insights on peak times, loyalty, and cross-department trends with clear business recommendations.

This project analyzes the Instacart Online Grocery Shopping Dataset 2017 to uncover shopping patterns, customer segments, and product demand. Using Python, Excel, and Tableau, the analysis transforms raw transactional data into actionable insights for marketing and operational strategies.

📌 Objectives

Identify peak shopping times and busiest days

Understand customer segmentation and loyalty behavior

Analyze product demand across departments and aisles

Provide business recommendations to improve marketing and retention

🛠️ Tools & Technologies

Python: pandas, NumPy, matplotlib, seaborn

Jupyter Notebook: exploratory analysis and documentation

Excel: pivot tables, cross-tabs, client-friendly deliverables

Tableau: interactive dashboards and storytelling

📂 Project Workflow

Data Cleaning & Preparation – standardization, missing values, merging multiple datasets

Exploratory Data Analysis – descriptive statistics, categorical plots, correlations

Customer Segmentation – profiling by frequency, loyalty, and region

Visualization & Storytelling – dashboards, hero charts, and insights in Tableau

Business Recommendations – practical strategies informed by data

📊 Key Insights

Evening hours are the busiest shopping times

Fresh produce and dairy dominate order frequency

Loyal customers show consistent reordering patterns

Regional differences create opportunities for targeted campaigns

🚀 Outcomes

Optimized marketing suggestions for peak hours

Customer profiles to guide targeted promotions

Cross-department trends that support upselling strategies
